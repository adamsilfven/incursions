# Rewards

Winning selects one weighted reward-table entry and rolls that table once. A table can produce several stacks. Rewards are shared physical drops by default, rather than a separate payout for each player. Failure does not award victory loot.

These percentages describe table selection after winning the corresponding incursion. They are not the chance of obtaining a particular item, and they do not describe the chance of an encounter occurring on a Minecraft day.

| Profile | Reward-table selection |
| --- | --- |
| Nether | Fortress selection 20%; bastion selection 30%; hoglin-stable selection 25%; vanilla bastion treasure 25% |
| Aether | Bronze selection 15%; silver selection 30%; native silver-dungeon reward 35%; native gold-dungeon reward 20% |
| Undergarden | Mine selection 25%; catacombs selection 50%; mound selection 25% |
| Twilight Forest | Room selection 35%; library selection 25%; potion selection 25%; cache selection 15% |

Incursions supplies curated tables alongside references to native premium loot. Datapacks and installed mod versions can affect the final items. The mod does not replace the dimension mods' original loot tables.
