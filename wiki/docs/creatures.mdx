# Creature Pools

Ordinary enemies are selected by weighted draws. These numbers are relative weights, not quotas. Age-capable incursion enemies are prepared as adults; unsupported entities that remain babies are rejected.

| Profile | Ordinary pool weights |
| --- | --- |
| Nether | Piglin 50; Zombified Piglin 15; Wither Skeleton 10; Piglin Brute 10; Blaze 10; Hoglin 5 |
| Aether | Cockatrice 20; Mimic 30; Sentry 45; Blue Swet 2.5; Golden Swet 2.5 |
| Undergarden | Rotling 25; Rotwalker 20; Nargoyle 20; Sploogie 20; Forgotten 15 |
| Twilight Forest | Helmet Crab 35; Slime Beetle 25; Minotaur 20; Block-and-Chain Goblin 10; Skeleton Druid 10 |

The Undergarden's rare Rotbeast is a separate opportunity rather than an ordinary pool entry. Ghasts, Zephyrs, and Redcaps are absent from the built-in pools.

Incursion Cockatrices have their inebriation effect suppressed. The infection-prevention setting applies to newly spawned incursion attackers; it does not cure existing Utheric Infection or change ordinary Undergarden enemies and biomes.
